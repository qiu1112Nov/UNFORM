A LITTLE MOVEMENT GOES A LONG WAY.
01 / WEAVE & UNRAVEL

Touch or hold near the sculpture. Particles gather into dashes, then fine threads travel across its triangular structure. Hold longer to reveal a few translucent facets. Swipe quickly to break nearby threads; particles gently return. Use two fingers to spread or gather.

02 / FLOW · FRAGMENT · TRACE

A small stream of particles travels along the edges. With the camera enabled, an open palm unfolds vertices, edges, then the interior; a fist rebuilds in reverse. Move your hand quickly for sparse local trails and a gentle swirl. Trails fade when you become still.

03 / MAKE IT YOURS

Hybrid motion preserves geometric anchors while fragments flow. Deconstruction sets the effect strength; an open palm or two fingers activates it. Lines, Circle and Grid remain available. Space pauses and 1–4 switches forms when no control is focused. Press play to weave when paused.

Camera processing stays in your browser. No video is recorded or uploaded. Move away from the controls to interact with the artwork.
